I'm Saroj Ratul, a third-year Electrical Engineering student at the University of Saskatchewan (Class of 2028) in the Engineering Co-op program.

This site holds build logs for projects I work on outside class. Each one is written while the work is happening, so it records the design decisions, the test results against their predictions, and the faults and how they were found, not only the parts that worked.

## Projects

- **[COMET Air Mouse](/comet-air-mouse/)**, in progress. A gyroscope-driven Bluetooth LE mouse, reworked into a classroom presenter remote.
- **[CalcFin20](/fc200v-emulator/)**, waiting on Google account verification. A native Android financial calculator, built as an alternative to the CASIO FC-200V so I could practise for an Engineering Economics midterm until the real calculator arrived.
- **[Wireless Bicycle Turn Signal](/turn-signal/)**, paused for the winter. A 32-LED backpack arrow commanded over ESP-NOW from a handlebar remote.

## Bench

A soldering station and fume extractor, a Digilent Analog Discovery 2 (oscilloscope, logic analyzer and waveform generator), a Jesverty SPS-6005N bench supply and a multimeter. Most of it was funded by bursaries and awards from my second year.
